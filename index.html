How it works

01

Cars come and go

Vehicles appear at an outer gateway, drive a multi-hour route through the junctions and leave through another gateway. Arrivals follow morning and evening rush hours.

02

4-hour warning

Four hours before a scheduled accident the Random Forest is told about it and forecasts how congestion will spread over the next 4 hours.

03

Stuck or rerouted

When the accident starts, cars less than 4 hours from it are stuck in the queue on the approach roads; cars 4 hours or more away are sent on different roads.

Important: the accidents are scheduled by you, so the system knows about them in advance. The Random Forest predicts how traffic will react to a known accident, learned from simulated data; it does not predict that a real accident will happen.
